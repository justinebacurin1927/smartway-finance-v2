How to Finance a Car in Queensland: A Complete 2026 Guide

Buying a car in Queensland? This step-by-step guide breaks down every finance option available — from secured loans to chattel mortgages — so you can drive away with confidence.

Buying a car is one of the biggest financial decisions most Australians make. Whether you're after a brand-new vehicle or a quality used car, understanding your finance options before setting foot in a dealership can save you thousands — and a lot of stress.

Step 1: Know Your Budget

Before you start browsing listings, get clear on what you can realistically afford. A good rule of thumb is to keep total vehicle costs — loan repayments, insurance, registration, fuel and maintenance — under 15–20% of your monthly take-home income.

Use our free loan calculator to model different scenarios. Getting a clear number upfront puts you in a stronger position at the dealership.

Step 2: Understand Your Finance Options

There are several ways to finance a car in Queensland. The right choice depends on whether you're buying for personal or business use, your credit profile, and how much flexibility you want.

Secured Car Loan

The most common option. The vehicle acts as security, typically resulting in lower rates and longer terms — up to 84 months. Works for new and used vehicles from dealers or private sellers.

Unsecured Personal Loan

If you prefer not to use the vehicle as collateral, an unsecured loan gives you full ownership from day one. Rates are generally a little higher to reflect the lender's additional risk.

Chattel Mortgage (Business)

For vehicles used primarily for business, a chattel mortgage lets you take ownership while the lender holds a security interest. There may be GST and tax deduction advantages — your accountant can advise on your specific situation.

Novated Lease

Available through your employer, a novated lease lets you bundle vehicle payments — and sometimes running costs — into your pre-tax salary. This can reduce taxable income, though the structure is more complex.

Step 3: Check Your Credit Score

Your credit score directly influences which lenders will approve you and at what rate. Check your credit report for free through Equifax or Illion before applying. Even small errors can affect your score — dispute anything that looks incorrect.

Protect Your Credit Score

Every formal loan application creates a 'hard enquiry' on your file, which temporarily lowers your score. A broker like Smartway Finance identifies the best-fit lender before applying — protecting your score from unnecessary hits.

Step 4: Compare Lenders — Not Just Rates

The headline interest rate is just one factor. The comparison rate (which includes fees and charges), repayment flexibility, early payout conditions, and the lender's approval speed all matter. Accessing 40+ lenders through a broker means you're comparing the market, not just the two or three options your bank offers.

Step 5: Get Pre-Approval

Pre-approval tells you exactly how much you can borrow before you start shopping. It puts you in a strong negotiating position — the dealer knows you're serious and ready to move. Most pre-approvals are valid for 30–90 days.

Step 6: Apply and Settle

Once you've found the right vehicle, your broker finalises the application, handles documentation (including PPSR checks for private sales), and manages settlement from end to end. For most clients, the whole process takes just a few days.

Bottom Line

Getting independent finance advice before you sign anything is one of the smartest moves you can make. A broker works for you — not the lender — comparing options across a wide panel to find the right fit for your situation.
