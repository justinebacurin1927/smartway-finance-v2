# Homepage Finance Hub layout

Primary: [Vero — Luxury Real Estate Insights CMS](https://dribbble.com/shots/27489413-Luxury-Real-Estate-Insights-CMS-Editorial-Blog-Experience).

The user rejected the cramped lead-story/sidebar composition and requested reference-led layout changes while retaining the video. Nine individual references and visual previews were reviewed; decisions are in journal-layout-sources.json.

Composition map: a two-line publication heading on a quiet paper background; broad spacing before the lead; a large native-proportion source photograph on the left, category/title/summary/metadata and a direct underlined reading link on the right; a fine divider and two broad supporting stories below with their copy beneath the photographs. Retain Smartway's established sans type and navy/sage roles. Adapt the reference's three lower stories to the three source articles total. No image overlays, portrait strips, cropping, arrow controls or new cards. Mobile stacks each full image and its complete article summary, with clear spacing between stories.

The existing hover/focus and reduced-motion behavior remains. No new runtime or gallery artwork. The hero markup and coastal video hash were verified unchanged. The canonical generator is enhance-site.cjs; refine-home-ending.cjs continues to own only FAQ/contact/footer.
